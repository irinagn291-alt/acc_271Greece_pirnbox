This is the first release of Pirnbox.

Today's bobbin shows remaining against capacity on a glass bobbin. Tap to stitch spends one unit. A tap at zero writes Bare and remaining stays at zero. Reload restores remaining to capacity and files a mark on the day. Retract peels the last stitch, Bare mark, or reload.

Calendar lists days with reload marks. Charts shows stitches versus capacity. History lists Stitch, Bare, and Reload writes. Capacity opens Stitch then reload. Settings sets Capacity from 1 to 48 and includes Contact Pirnbox, Re-run onboarding, and Reset all data.

Onboarding is Thread on the glass, Tap to stitch, and Reload the spool.

No account is required.
