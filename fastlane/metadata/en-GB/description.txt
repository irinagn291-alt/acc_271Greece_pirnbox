Pirnbox is a remaining-thread log for home sewers. Today's bobbin shows remaining against capacity on a glass bobbin, not a stitch diary.

• Today's bobbin shows Remaining thread on the glass and Capacity
• Tap to stitch spends one unit from remaining
• At zero, a tap writes Bare and remaining stays at zero
• Reload restores remaining to capacity and files a mark on the day
• Retract peels the last stitch, Bare mark or reload
• Calendar lists days with reload marks
• Charts shows stitches versus capacity
• History lists Stitch, Bare and Reload writes
• Stitch then reload follows the same mechanic from the Capacity card
• Settings sets Capacity from 1 to 48, plus Contact Pirnbox, Re-run onboarding and Reset all data

There is no account. Remaining and marks stay on this device.
